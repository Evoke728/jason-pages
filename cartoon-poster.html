女版
霓虹藍運動服。複製整段，連同你的照片一起丟給 AI。
先上傳你的照片，再貼這段
Create a hyper-realistic tiny planet fisheye scene featuring a young woman standing happily at the center of a miniature 3D world.

Use the uploaded photo as reference. Preserve her exact facial likeness, expression, and hairstyle — keep it clearly recognizable as her, no distortion.

She wears a neon blue sweatshirt, matching sweatpants, and white sneakers, standing confidently at the top of a small curved planet with a strong tiny-planet / little-planet fisheye effect.

Fill the miniature world around her with cheerful animated companions — playful characters in the nostalgic style of early-2000s cartoons (the Cartoon Network / Disney Channel / Jetix / Pogo / Hungama / Nickelodeon vibe) — as if she is the protagonist of a nostalgic cartoon universe.

Style: hyper-realistic tiny planet composition, ultra-detailed 3D animated rendering blended with a cinematic DSLR photography look, vibrant nostalgic colors, dramatic lighting, 8K ultra quality.
男版
深綠運動服。其餘設定與女版相同。
先上傳你的照片，再貼這段
Create a hyper-realistic tiny planet fisheye scene featuring a young man standing happily at the center of a miniature 3D world.

Use the uploaded photo as reference. Preserve his exact facial likeness, expression, and hairstyle — keep it clearly recognizable as him, no distortion.

He wears a dark green sweatshirt, matching sweatpants, and white sneakers, standing confidently at the top of a small curved planet with a strong tiny-planet / little-planet fisheye effect.

Fill the miniature world around him with cheerful animated companions — playful characters in the nostalgic style of early-2000s cartoons (the Cartoon Network / Disney Channel / Jetix / Pogo / Hungama / Nickelodeon vibe) — as if he is the protagonist of a nostalgic cartoon universe.

Style: hyper-realistic tiny planet composition, ultra-detailed 3D animated rendering blended with a cinematic DSLR photography look, vibrant nostalgic colors, dramatic lighting, 8K ultra quality.
想做出不一樣的，改這幾個地方
  • 換衣服顏色：把 neon blue / dark green 換成你想要的（例如 bright red、pastel pink）
  • 換年代 / 風格：把「early-2000s cartoons」換成你愛的——90 年代日系動畫、迪士尼經典、像素遊戲風
  • 換場景：tiny planet 可以改成 city street、school rooftop、神社、賽博龐克城市
  • 加標語：結尾加一句 with a bold poster title that says「你的文字」at the top，做成可發限動的海報
